Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find my license key?

Your license key is included in your purchase confirmation email from Lemon Squeezy. Search your inbox for "Mandrain" if you can't find it.

How do I activate the extension?

Click the Mandrain icon in your Chrome toolbar, enter your license key in the popup, then click Activate. The extension will be ready immediately.

Can I use the extension on multiple devices?

Each license key supports one device at a time. If you need more devices, contact us.

The card isn't appearing when I select Chinese text — what should I try?

Make sure the extension is enabled for the current site (click the icon → check site permissions). Reload the page after enabling. If the issue persists, email us with the URL of the page you were on.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from your Lemon Squeezy customer portal. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
