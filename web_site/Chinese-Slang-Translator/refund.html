Refund Policy

Chinese Slang Translator · Last updated: March 17, 2026 · Published by Gecktool

Subscription model

Chinese Slang Translator is offered as a monthly subscription at $4.99 / month. Subscriptions are billed monthly and renew automatically until cancelled.

Refund eligibility

SituationEligibility
Request within 7 days of first payment, extension did not work as described Full refund
Request within 7 days, changed your mind Full refund
Request after 7 days but within 30 days, documented technical issue we could not resolve Case-by-case
Request after 30 days Not eligible
Renewal charge you forgot to cancel One-time courtesy refund if requested within 3 days

How to request a refund

Submit a refund request via our support form: select "Refund request" as the topic and include:

  • The email address used for your subscription
  • Date of purchase
  • Brief reason for the request

We will respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days depending on your payment provider.

Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation stops future charges; you retain access until the end of the current billing period. Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund for the current period unless it falls within the 7-day window above.
